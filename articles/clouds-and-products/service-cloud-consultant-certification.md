# Agentforce Service Consultant Certification

The **Salesforce Certified Agentforce Service Consultant** credential is Salesforce's consultant-tier certification for Service Cloud. Trailhead describes holders as experts at designing and implementing Service Cloud solutions that are sustainable and scalable, meet business requirements and support long-term customer success.[^s1] The credential was long known as the **Salesforce Certified Service Cloud Consultant**. Its old Trailhead address still loads, but it now serves the Agentforce-branded page and names the new URL as canonical.[^s2] This node covers both names, because they are one certification and not two credentials to earn.

## The name change

Salesforce has renamed certifications in more than one wave. An earlier round, covering 30-plus credentials, left the Service Cloud Consultant name unchanged. That round told holders they would not need to re-earn anything, because profiles and verification records update automatically.[^s6] In June 2026 Salesforce's certification team announced a second round renaming 16 certifications, effective July 24, 2026, and pointed holders to a name-change FAQ.[^s3] The credential now carries the Agentforce Service name on Trailhead, matching how Salesforce markets Service Cloud today.[^s1][^s2] Search for both names when you look for prep material or check a candidate's record. The official prep trailmix keeps a URL slug from the old name.[^s1]

## Prerequisite and exam

The credential page lists **Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator** as the prerequisite to earn first.[^s1] It gives no exam format. Instead it links to a Trailhead Academy exam page whose identifier is Service-Con-201, so confirm the current question count, duration, fee and passing score there before booking.[^s1]

A practitioner study guide last updated in April 2025, written before the Agentforce rename, describes the exam then. It lists 60 multiple-choice questions, a $200 fee, a passing score of 67% and a time limit of 1 hour 45 minutes.[^s5] It also names the "Salesforce Administrator" credential as the prerequisite, which predates the current Platform Administrator wording on Trailhead.[^s1][^s5] Treat these figures as secondary.

## Exam scope

The same guide says four heavily weighted sections make up more than 60% of the exam:[^s5]

- **Service Cloud solution design (20%)**: customer requirements, solution design, channel use cases and security design for compliance.[^s5]
- **Case management (18%)**: case assignment, escalation and resolution, plus entitlements, milestones, assets and service-level agreements.[^s5]
- **Intake and interaction channels (15%)**: deploying intake channels and designing interaction channels, which the guide calls a newer section.[^s5]
- **Implementation strategies (14%)**: planning, requirements, design, build, testing, documentation, data migration and quality, contact center licensing, deployment and training.[^s5]

The guide lists smaller areas without weights: industry knowledge, knowledge management, contact center analytics and integrations.[^s5] For hands-on practice it suggests the Service Cloud Specialist superbadge alongside official Trailhead material.[^s5]

## Maintenance

Salesforce credentials need an annual Trailhead maintenance badge, released in Spring, Summer and Winter cycles.[^s3] The **Maintain Your Salesforce Certifications** trail lists modules for each of the Spring, Summer and Winter '26 cycles. None of them is named for Service Cloud or Agentforce Service Consultant, though the Spring '26 list includes Platform Administrator.[^s4] Check your Trailhead profile, or Salesforce's maintenance schedule, to see which module and due date apply to this credential. Don't assume it has its own badge.[^s3][^s4]

## Gotchas

- **One credential, two names.** Résumés, partner records and older guides still say "Service Cloud Consultant". It is the same certification as Agentforce Service Consultant.[^s1][^s2]
- **Older guides use older names.** Pre-rename material may cite the Salesforce Administrator prerequisite and pre-Agentforce product names. Check against the live credential page.[^s1][^s5]
- **Case design dominates.** Case lifecycle, SLAs and channel design together outweigh any single implementation topic in the published outline.[^s5]

# Platform Administrator Certification

The **Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator** is the foundational credential for people who configure and run Salesforce orgs. Trailhead pitches it at professionals who help their companies get more out of the Salesforce Platform by putting additional features and capabilities to work.[^s1] Older material, prep content and job ads often call it simply the "Salesforce Certified Administrator" or the administrator certification; the credential page, the prep trail and the Trailhead Academy exam page (code Plat-Admn-201) all point at the same certification, so treat those names as one subject.[^s1][^s4] It sits in Trailhead's Administrator role track beside Platform Administrator II, Platform App Builder, Agentforce Specialist, Business Analyst, Marketing Cloud Engagement Administrator and Slack Administrator.[^s5]

## Who it is for

Trailhead's official prep module says the exam is aimed at people who already have hands-on platform administration experience, and that it spans the Sales, Service and collaboration applications as well as core setup.[^s3] In practice that means the person who manages users and security, customises objects and page layouts, maintains data quality, builds declarative automation and supports business users day to day. Builders who mainly design custom declarative applications have a separate, adjacent credential in Platform App Builder.[^s5]

## Exam outline (December 2025 refresh)

Salesforce refreshed the exam effective December 15, 2025. The Salesforce Admins blog describes the change as fine-tuning rather than an overhaul: core admin skills stay, weights shift and AI knowledge is added.[^s2] The current weighting, as published by both the blog and the Trailhead prep module, is:[^s2][^s3]

| Section | Weight |
|---|---|
| Configuration and Setup | 15% |
| Object Manager and Lightning App Builder | 15% |
| Sales and Marketing Applications | 10% |
| Service and Support Applications | 10% |
| Productivity and Collaboration | 10% |
| Data and Analytics Management | 17% |
| Automation | 15% |
| Agentforce | 8%[^s2][^s3] |

The new **Agentforce** section covers when AI is appropriate, Agentforce use cases, security and troubleshooting agent permissions, and maintaining or installing prompts and instructions in Agent Builder with light testing and conversation preview. The blog stresses that expert-level Agentforce knowledge is not expected.[^s2] Data and analytics is now the heaviest single section, so reports, dashboards, data import and data quality deserve proportionate study time.[^s2]

Question count, duration, passing score and fees are published in the official exam guide that the prep module links to; they were not re-verified for this article, so check that guide before booking.[^s3] The exam is delivered either online-proctored or in person at a testing centre.[^s3]

## Preparation

The official **Study for the Administrator Certification Exam** trail has three cert-prep modules (Configuration, Setup and Objects; Applications and Activities; Data and Automation) with scenarios and flashcards, about 1 hour 20 minutes in total.[^s4] Trailhead recommends Salesforce-approved material such as exam guides, trails, trailmixes, Trailhead Academy courses and Trailhead LIVE, plus study groups in the Trailblazer Community, and warns candidates not to share or request exam questions or superbadge solutions.[^s3] The credential page links a curated preparation trailmix.[^s1] The admins blog points to the Object Relationships and Flow Administration superbadges as hands-on practice for the refreshed exam.[^s2]

## Maintenance and gotchas

- **Annual maintenance.** Holders must complete release maintenance modules once a year or the certification expires.[^s3] The current cycle's module is *Platform Administrator Certification Maintenance (Spring '26)*, a roughly 10-minute badge on the Maintain Your Salesforce Certifications trail.[^s7]
- **It gates other credentials.** Platform Administrator II lists this certification as its prerequisite, so a lapse here undermines the advanced credential too.[^s6]
- **Exam refreshes change the blueprint.** Prep material written before December 2025 has no Agentforce section and older weights; prefer Trailhead's current prep modules.[^s2][^s3]

## When to pursue it

It is the natural first credential for admins, business analysts moving into configuration, and consultants who will need product-specific consultant exams later.[^s1][^s5] Experienced admins who already hold it move on to Platform Administrator II, which Trailhead describes as for seasoned administrators who apply advanced features to business problems.[^s5][^s6] People whose work is mostly custom declarative app design may pair it with, or prioritise, Platform App Builder.[^s5]

# Data 360 Consultant Certification

The **Salesforce Certified Data 360 Consultant** is Salesforce's credential for people who implement and consult on enterprise data platforms in a customer-facing role, applied to Salesforce's own data platform.[^s1] It launched on September 6, 2023 as the Data Cloud Consultant certification, after Salesforce opened what had been a partner-only accreditation to any Salesforce professional.[^s6] Salesforce rebranded Data Cloud as Data 360 on October 14, 2025, stating that functionality was unchanged and that the old name would linger in the app and docs during the transition.[^s5] The credential followed: Trailhead now lists it under the Data 360 name, while some Trailhead module URLs and titles still say "Data Cloud".[^s1][^s4]

## Who it is for

Trailhead's prep module aims the exam at people in development, strategy, business analysis, pre-sales solutioning and architecture roles, and recommends two or more years of hands-on data strategy and data modeling experience, including building solutions across several Salesforce clouds.[^s3] No prerequisite is required, though the module suggests holding the Salesforce Administrator credential first.[^s3] The credential page lists the Agentforce Specialist, Platform Administrator and Platform App Builder certifications as related credentials.[^s1]

## Exam format and outline

The prep module gives the exam as 60 questions with a 62% passing score, a $200 fee, results shown immediately, delivery online or at a test centre, and no reference materials allowed.[^s3] Its outline (written under the Data Cloud name) weights six sections: overview 18%, setup and administration 12%, data ingestion and modeling 20%, identity resolution 14%, segmentation and insights 18%, and acting on data 18%.[^s3][^s6] The credential page itself shows no format details, and the trail points candidates to the official Data 360 Consultant Exam Guide for current exam specifics, so check that guide when you register.[^s1][^s2]

The current prep trail (about 13 hours 40 minutes) shows how the scope has moved with the product. Beyond ingestion, harmonization, unification and segmentation, it covers credit consumption, data spaces and governance, packaging with data kits, zero-copy connectors, streaming and batch transforms, Tableau Next and AI models, Agentforce integrations, and hands-on projects with unstructured data and data graphs.[^s2] That spread matches what the platform does: connect and ingest structured and unstructured data, harmonize it to a standard model, unify profiles with identity resolution rulesets, build insights, segment and activate audiences, and ground Agentforce.[^s5]

## Maintenance

Holders must complete a certification maintenance module once a year.[^s3] The Winter '26 module, for example, is a short unit with a due date set by Salesforce's release maintenance schedule. It covered Hyperforce expansion, real-time data actions, the move of segmentation and activation credits to a consumption card in Digital Wallet, new API and ingestion limits, multi-org connections, configuration migration across orgs and streaming ingestion APIs.[^s4]

## Gotchas

- **Two names in circulation.** Look for both "Data Cloud Consultant" and "Data 360 Consultant" when searching prep material or listing the credential; it is one certification, not two.[^s1][^s5]
- **Older weightings.** The published section weightings come from the Data Cloud-era prep module and launch coverage. The newer trail is organised differently, so confirm the current outline in the exam guide.[^s2][^s3]
- **Consumption is in scope.** The trail treats credit consumption and the Digital Wallet as consultant knowledge, not only as billing detail, so expect to reason about cost as well as configuration.[^s2][^s4]
